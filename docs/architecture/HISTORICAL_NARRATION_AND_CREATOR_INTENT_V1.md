# Historical Narration and Creator Intent v1

## Purpose

This layer persists Abdulrahman Akah's editorial reasoning for SIRAJ so later
episodes and later conversations do not restart calibration from zero. It is an
editorial policy layer, not an evidence approval layer.

## Core rules

1. Do not assert firstness, exclusivity, or an exact causal sequence unless sound
   evidence establishes that exact claim.
2. Trace the origin of non-Quranic details before choosing narration certainty.
3. Useful Israiliyyat may be retained when non-contradictory, but must be
   explicitly labeled and narrated non-assertively.
4. A supported synthesis is allowed when at least two premises are established,
   the premise trace is retained, and the conclusion adds no unproved detail.
5. Preserve complete source trace internally, but do not overload the spoken
   narration with source names. Audience attribution becomes mandatory at
   doctrinally sensitive, morally serious, disputed, weak, conflicting, or
   Israiliyyat points.
6. Unknown specifics must remain unknown in both narration and visuals.
