# Adam High-Importance Evidence Resolution v1

This stage records the creator's explicit human decisions for the three
high-importance sources and six complex external-source events.

Key resolutions:

- The Pen is narrated assertively as the first created thing according to the
  explicit hadith wording.
- Iblis's individual existence before the beginning of Adam's creation may be
  mentioned only as a qualified tafsir report.
- Quranic descriptions of Adam's material are asserted, while a detailed
  chronological sequence remains qualified.
- The Quranic teaching of the names is asserted. Documented tafsir views may be
  mentioned with attribution and without exclusive certainty; Israiliyyat
  require an explicit label.
- The progeny-from-Adam report is included with its recorded grade but is not
  made the sole interpretation of the covenant verse.
- Hawwa's name and the supported synthesis that she was created from Adam's rib
  are restored from the earlier approved evidence. Left-rib, sleep, healing,
  dialogue, angel question, and name-etymology details remain qualified tafsir
  or explicitly labelled Israiliyyat.

The fourteen external-source event scopes are complete. Broader integration
with all thirty-seven episode events and final approved evidence-package
construction remain pending. The evidence gate and providers remain blocked.
