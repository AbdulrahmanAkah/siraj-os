This stage creates the full human-review candidate for episode 001 after the
approved evidence gate has opened.

Deliverables:

- complete Arabic cinematic narration for a 22-minute episode;
- fourteen evidence-bound dramatic sequences preserving the bound frame order;
- seventy timed shots with composition, camera, action, light, sound,
  transition role, treatment, and religious visual-safety instructions;
- complete trace coverage of all 37 events and all 57 evidence items;
- a production-planning brief with provider, budget, and generated-video
  execution deferred;

The creative identity is a world-class prestige historical cinematic series.
Dry documentary, lecture, slideshow, stock montage, flat chronology, literal
depiction of the unseen, depiction of Allah or angels, depiction of prophets
as literal faces or bodies, and invented historical dialogue are forbidden.

The package performs no live, direct, paid, or Runware execution.
