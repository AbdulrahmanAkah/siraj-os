# Adam Research and Production Policy Bundle v1

This change integrates four previously separate concerns into one deterministic,
offline stage:

1. temporary hadith verification through Dorar al-Sunniyyah with exact scholar
   and source attribution and no automated grading;
2. contextual and symbolic cinematic filler that may improve pacing without
   changing historical events or turning possible context into fact;
3. Quran recitation cues using Mishary Rashid Alafasy as the preferred reciter,
   with a preference for live prayer recordings from 1998–2010;
4. a targeted evidence review pack for EV-ADAM-031, EV-ADAM-071, and
   EV-ADAM-091 using existing local review artifacts as an automated prefilter
   only.

## Audio law

Music is prohibited globally. During Quran recitation the Quran is the only
audible program element: narrator, ambience, and sound effects are muted.

## Filler law

Battle preparations, travel preparations, and social conditions may be shown
only after the relevant period/context is verified. They may not imply that a
specific preparation, person, action, dialogue, location, or chronology belongs
to the historical event unless evidence supports it.

Paradise and Hell may be approached visually as conceptual approximations of
extreme beauty or punishment. The images must never claim to reproduce their
true form, assign a specific person to either destination, or establish a
doctrinal duration claim by visuals alone.

## Evidence law

The generated review pack does not extract or approve evidence. It identifies
candidate records and produces strict NotebookLM instructions. Exact extraction,
source verification, classification, and final disposition remain manual.

## Rights law

The user's expectation that older Alafasy recordings are reusable is recorded,
but the system does not convert that expectation into a legal conclusion.
Publication remains blocked until a publication basis is recorded.
