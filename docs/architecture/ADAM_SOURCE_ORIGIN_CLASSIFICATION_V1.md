# Adam Source-Origin Classification v1

## Purpose

This layer records the manual origin classification reached after reviewing the
three open Adam research gaps. It does not approve evidence and does not open the
evidence gate.

## EV-ADAM-031

The sound claim is narrowed to Adam's sneeze and praise of Allah after the soul
was breathed into him. The layer prohibits calling the sneeze Adam's first
movement or the praise his first speech unless a sound source establishes that
exact firstness.

## EV-ADAM-071

The layer separates four different levels:

1. The name Hawwa is established by authentic hadith.
2. The woman was created from a rib in authentic hadith.
3. "Hawwa was created from Adam's rib" is a supported synthesis of those two
   premises and adds no left-rib, sleep, or dialogue detail.
4. The report that Adam was alone in Paradise and had no spouse in whom to find
   repose is found in al-Tabari 8406, Ibn Abi Hatim 8276, and al-Bayhaqi 820
   through the al-Suddi tafsir material. It is a tafsir report, not a marfu hadith.
   The composite chain mixes origins that are not distinguishable, so the layer
   does not falsely label the whole report definite Israiliyyat and does not
   narrate it assertively.

Ibn Ishaq's left-rib, sleep, and flesh-restoration details are separately marked
as explicit Israiliyyat because the transmitted text itself says that it came
from the People of the Book.
