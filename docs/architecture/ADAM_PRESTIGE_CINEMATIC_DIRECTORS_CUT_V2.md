# Adam Prestige Cinematic Director’s Cut v2

This revision implements the creator’s explicit policy:

> Source wording and source context are not the presentation format. The
> supported meaning must be preserved, while the expression is transformed
> into cinematic drama, image, sound, rhythm, and authored staging.

The v2 candidate preserves the approved 37-event and 57-evidence trace, all
seven qualification boundaries, the open evidence gate, and every religious
visual-safety rule. It does not alter evidence meaning or confidence.

Editorial changes:

- the opening enters directly through the prostration conflict;
- methodology exposition is removed from narration;
- phrases such as “we mention the report”, “the scholars differed”, and
  “according to the recorded grade” are removed from the voice-over;
- necessary qualifications are integrated into dramatic language and
  on-screen visual notes;
- the material world dominates the storyboard: primordial water, terrain,
  weather, clay, valleys, gardens, human history, and environmental response;
- the v1 script and storyboard remain preserved as superseded audit artifacts;
- no paid, live, direct, or Runware execution is authorised.

The target remains 22 minutes, fourteen evidence-bound sequences, and seventy
timed shots. Human approval remains required before master visual design or an
unpaid animatic.


## Idempotent episode-definition rebuild

`update_episode_definition()` is idempotent. Once Director's Cut v2 is
installed, rebuilding from the resulting episode definition preserves the
original v1 supersession audit record instead of recapturing v2 as though
it were the superseded predecessor. The publisher runs materialization
once and a non-materializing rebuild immediately afterward before tests or
publication.
